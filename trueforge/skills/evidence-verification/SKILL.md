# Evidence Verification

Do not promote a candidate issue to VERIFIED solely from model judgment. Reproduce serious findings in the sandbox. Capture input, tool calls, remote state where available, policy/approval events and final result. For critical findings, attempt at least two reproductions. Reject findings that cannot be supported by observable behavior. Clearly distinguish expected, observed and inferred state.
